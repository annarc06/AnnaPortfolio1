Biography

From rural Illinois, I grew up on a farm in a family of creatives and hard workers. Ever since I was a pre-teen and heard the term 'designer,' I knew that was what I wanted to be. At first, I pursued the idea of a graphic designer but that shortly expanded into web design, then with its final evolution: full stack web development. It has a nice ring to it, right? Ever since, it has been my mission to develop and hone the skills of both code and design to create sleek, inspiring, webpages for anyone who asks.

Ambitions

I have big dreams, and I intend to fulfill them through whatever it necessary. This fosters an enduring work ethic, energy to try new things, and an openness to critique, new paths, and other ways to approach challenges. Completing my life-long dream of working as a web developer will be a challenge, but one I am willing to surmount. Much like that, I am willing to surmount any other challenges that come my way whether in the form of a difficult and complex project or making effort to develop the perfect brand color scheme that screams 'made for you!'
